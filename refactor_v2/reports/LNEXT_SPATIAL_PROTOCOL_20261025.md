# L spatial pilot, prospective specification

This is the second user-authorized architecture study, after the current V study. Preparation may run during V; L model gates and training wait for V execution and analysis to finish. Results are restricted to the inspected development cohort.

Four arms: L_real, L_placebo, L_uniform, L_off. Each uses the same original-image B encoder and an identical small LayerNorm/linear patch head. All use stage3 QA training throughout, seed42, the original 256 TRAIN / 1164 QA and 321 model-validation / 1432 QA manifests, four epochs / 292 updates and the same schedules, upstream zero-task initialization, optimizer and losses as the current new B. Spatial loss weight is fixed at0.1 for real/placebo/uniform and0 for off. This replaces the old lens-view alignment mechanism with simultaneous spatial auxiliary learning; it does not reproduce the old two-phase L.

Real targets are existing TRAIN union boxes, mapped through EXIF canonicalization and the exact rounded224px letterbox geometry. A14x14 target records fractional union-box coverage in valid image pixels; padding is excluded from loss. These are weak box targets, not pixel segmentation labels. Only the one canonical fracture-presence QA per TRAIN image contributes spatial loss. Negative images have empty boxes. No clinical label is invented.

Placebo cyclically shifts the box-union raster by half the resized image width and height within the valid image rectangle. It preserves image-level foreground mass and the valid image mask, but may split boxes at the border and may overlap the true location. Record true/placebo overlap. Uniform assigns the same foreground mass uniformly across valid image pixels, controlling image-level presence/area supervision without spatial localization. Positive loss weight is derived once from total real TRAIN foreground/background pixel mass and shared by all spatial arms. No model-validation target determines training weights.

Spatial loss updates the shared original encoder. Patch-head output does not feed fusion, fracture logits, CLOSED logits or OPEN prefix. Public inference receives original images and questions; it does not require boxes, masks, proposals or spatial targets. At a fixed checkpoint, changing/removing spatial metadata and head intervention must leave all public answers/probabilities exactly unchanged. This makes any predictive change across training arms attributable to shared-feature learning under the auxiliary objective, subject to the controls.
